Michal Krbec

I am a second-year cybersecurity student at SSPŠ in Prague. I enjoy working in C++ because it lets me turn mathematics and algorithms into real systems whose behaviour I can measure, test, and improve. Search, simulations, and code below the interface are usually the most interesting parts for me.

My system is Arch Linux, and I change it often. I am the kind of technician who, instead of downloading a program that does something for me, spends twenty hours editing a configuration file just to get one nice animation.

At Lex Defensio Initiative (LDI), I select components, build hardware, and program solutions for FPV systems. I have also worked on an FPV video-signal relay. You can read more on the LDI website.
